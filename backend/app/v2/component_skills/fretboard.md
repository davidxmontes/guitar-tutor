# Positions across the neck

Connect pitch, interval and physical location. Use a small range for a first playable task; widen for transposition or connecting positions.

Reads: Focus, tuning, tonal center, kept note groups.

Actions and selection: Select a degree in Harmony or a step in Progression; change labels/range; audition and practise notes.

Pairs well with: circle-of-fifths, scale-staff, chord-diagram.

Layout and configuration: Use at least 280px; a full-width row is best. The neck scrolls inside its own viewport. Do not use a full neck when teaching one shape.
