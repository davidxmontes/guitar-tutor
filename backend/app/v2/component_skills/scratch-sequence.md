# Try a chord sequence

Audition a short ordered sequence before developing a timed Progression idea.

Reads: Scratch chords and focus chord.

Actions and selection: Add, remove, reorder, hear/loop, Develop.

Pairs well with: chord-palette, fretboard.

Layout and configuration: Medium or full width. It has no timing or assigned voicings; use Progression for those.
