# Diatonic chord choices

Connect the scale to its chords and Roman numerals. Selecting a chord preserves the tonal center.

Reads: Tonal center, scale, selected chord.

Actions and selection: Select chord; add chord to scratch.

Pairs well with: circle-of-fifths, fretboard, chord-inspector.

Layout and configuration: Compact wrapping row. labels is symbols or numerals. Non-heptatonic scales may have no diatonic palette.
