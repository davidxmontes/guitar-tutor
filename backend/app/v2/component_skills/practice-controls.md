# Practice controls

These controls are embedded in the fretboard and persistent Progression strip; they are not a separate layout block. Teach a short drill with tempo, duration, and a concrete self-check. The learner controls tempo, count-in, looping and audio. Playback position is transient and must not overwrite Focus. Pair the containing fretboard or progression view with one concise explanation. Never claim to have heard or assessed the learner.
