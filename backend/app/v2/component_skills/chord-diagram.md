# One physical shape

Teach which strings and frets actually sound. Open circles mean open strings; crosses mean muted strings. Selection uses trusted positions, never generated fingering.

Reads: Selected voicing or selected progression step; tuning.

Actions and selection: Click the diagram to select; Hear auditions the exact shape. Harmony offers Pin and Compare through the voicing strip.

Pairs well with: fretboard, chord-inspector, voice-leading.

Layout and configuration: Use beside a fretboard. In Progression subject may be focus, previous or next; omitted subject shows the selected transition pair. Do not claim a pitch-class set is a playable fingering.

For several distinct diagrams, Harmony subject may be {"voicing_label": "exact label from resolved voicings"}; Progression may use {"step_id": "existing step ID"}. Unknown references show no shape. Never supply physical coordinates in layout config.
