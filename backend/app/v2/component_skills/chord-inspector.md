# Chord construction and extensions

Explain the selected chord through spelled tones and intervals. Compare a triad with a seventh or extension by selecting chord quality while preserving the key. Explain any omitted tones in a physical shape.

Reads: Focus chord, chord notes, function in key.

Actions and selection: Explore the chord; Progression can replace it.

Pairs well with: chord-diagram, voicing-explorer, fretboard.

Layout and configuration: Compact supporting space. Its subject follows selection. No playable voicing may exist for every supported quality/tuning; do not invent one.
