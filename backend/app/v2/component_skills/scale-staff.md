# Compact spelled scale strip

Show the ordered scale notes and interval labels together. This is a compact pitch strip, not conventional engraved staff notation. For Dorian versus natural minor, emphasize the sixth and explain its sound.

Reads: Tonal center, scale, selected degree.

Actions and selection: Select a note/degree to synchronize fretboard highlighting.

Pairs well with: circle-of-fifths, fretboard.

Layout and configuration: Small or medium, wraps on mobile. labels is notes or degrees. Avoid doubling degree-map.
